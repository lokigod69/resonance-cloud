# Guided Today A1P6-A1P10 Spine Extension - 2026-05-15

## Scope

This is a docs-only curriculum spine proposal. It does not create runtime data.

Active pair remains German -> English. The level remains A1 practical: short phrases, concrete situations, no assumed speech scoring, no category practice, no other target languages, and no backend or Supabase work.

## Recommendation

Implement `English A1 Practical 6 - Health, Pharmacy, Small Needs` first after approval.

Reason: P6 fills a high-utility A1 survival gap that is adjacent to P1-P5 without becoming A2. It reuses existing repair, help, direction, waiting, and choice language while adding concrete health/pharmacy vocabulary. P7-P10 should remain spine-only until P6 is authored, tested, and reviewed.

## Trophy-Word Direction

New trophies should stay concrete and A1-usable. Prefer nouns and short pragmatic adjectives that fit a real beginner interaction:

- P6: health item, symptom, risk, recovery word.
- P7: hotel object, stay action, room-state word.
- P8: contact/detail, form, phone, send/receive word.
- P9: social choice, preference, limit, positive close word.
- P10: consolidation words that connect already learned moments.

Avoid celebrating baseline politeness words such as `please`, `hello`, or `sorry` as trophy words. They can appear in phrases, but they should not be the trophy anchor.

## Reuse Policy

Reuse should be intentional and lesson-scoped:

- Reuse high-utility repair words such as `again`, `slowly`, `helped`, `wait`, and `clear` where the new situation needs them.
- Reuse planning words such as `tomorrow`, `plan`, `settled`, and `done` in P9/P10 only when the phrase stays A1.
- Reuse location words such as `here`, `nearby`, `right`, and `place` to keep continuity across transport, health, hotel, and forms.
- Do not reuse a P1-P5 trophy word as a new trophy anchor in the same semantic role unless the path is explicitly consolidating that word.
