# Guided Today Vibe Character Bibles

Date: 2026-05-11

Bright, Wistful, and Sharp are V0 active. Tender, Bold, and Cheeky are future expansion bibles and must not appear as selectable launch UI until product promotes them.

## Active: Bright

Bright is clear, warm, upbeat, and easy to repeat. It sounds like a friendly person opening a door. It should be practical first, lightly optimistic second.

- Personality: helpful, polite, simple, socially warm
- Avoid: childish excitement, filler jokes, marketing cheer
- Word palette: `please`, `hello`, `easy`, `sure`, `great`, `smile`
- Trophy candidates: `please`, `hello`, `easy`, `sure`, `great`, `smile`
- Signature phrasings: `Excuse me, ...`, `Could you ...?`, `That helps.`, `Thanks so much.`
- Example phrases: `Excuse me, do you speak English?`, `Great, thank you.`
- Scene mood: daylight, open posture, helpful first-contact energy
- UI aesthetic: warm white space, clear contrast, soft yellow accents, minimal friction
- Music note: light acoustic pop

## Active: Wistful

Wistful is soft, reflective, and gentle without becoming unusably poetic. It sounds like a quiet traveler trying to be understood.

- Personality: reflective, humble, human, slightly vulnerable
- Avoid: melodrama, literary phrasing, phrases an A1 learner cannot use
- Word palette: `sorry`, `quiet`, `home`, `lost`, `softly`, `again`
- Trophy candidates: `sorry`, `quiet`, `home`, `lost`, `softly`, `again`
- Signature phrasings: `Sorry, ...`, `Could you say that again?`, `I am a little lost.`, `That feels familiar.`
- Example phrases: `Sorry, do you speak English?`, `Could you say that again?`
- Scene mood: evening light, quieter pacing, a small moment of courage before speaking
- UI aesthetic: muted blue-gray surfaces, gentle highlights, spacious rhythm, calm motion
- Music note: soft indie folk

## Active: Sharp

Sharp is direct, crisp, and efficient. It is for learners who want momentum and clean intent.

- Personality: concise, focused, confident, low-friction
- Avoid: rudeness, commands without courtesy, corporate bluntness
- Word palette: `clear`, `now`, `ready`, `fast`, `exact`, `focus`
- Trophy candidates: `clear`, `now`, `ready`, `fast`, `exact`, `focus`
- Signature phrasings: `Can you ...?`, `Say it again.`, `Clear enough.`, `Ready.`
- Example phrases: `Can you speak English?`, `Say it again, please.`
- Scene mood: high-contrast framing, efficient movement, no wasted words
- UI aesthetic: sharper contrast, compact controls, clean dark/light edges, precise states
- Music note: minimal synth pulse

## Future: Tender

Tender is caring, patient, and emotionally safe. It should support learners who want reassurance and warmth.

- Personality: patient, reassuring, safe, kind
- Avoid: infantilizing the learner or adding too much emotional copy
- Word palette: `kind`, `gentle`, `care`, `safe`, `together`, `slowly`
- Trophy candidates: `kind`, `gentle`, `care`, `safe`, `together`, `slowly`
- Signature phrasings: `It is okay.`, `Slowly, please.`, `Thank you for helping.`, `I am learning.`
- Example phrases: `Slowly, please.`, `Thank you for helping.`
- UI aesthetic: soft rose and cream accents, reassuring spacing, low-pressure feedback
- Music note: warm piano ballad

## Future: Bold

Bold is confident, energetic, and forward-moving. It should help learners practice with more presence and agency.

- Personality: confident, energetic, clear, action-oriented
- Avoid: aggressive phrasing, overlong hype, phrases that exceed A1
- Word palette: `ready`, `strong`, `go`, `lead`, `voice`, `again`
- Trophy candidates: `ready`, `strong`, `go`, `lead`, `voice`, `again`
- Signature phrasings: `I can try.`, `Let us go.`, `Say it again.`, `I am ready.`
- Example phrases: `I am ready.`, `Say it again, please.`
- UI aesthetic: confident contrast, larger action states, saturated accent moments
- Music note: anthemic pop rock

## Future: Cheeky

Cheeky is playful, quick, and lightly mischievous. It should make phrases memorable without becoming slang-heavy or mean.

- Personality: playful, quick, memorable, light
- Avoid: sarcasm, insults, taboo language, culturally brittle jokes
- Word palette: `oops`, `nice`, `tiny`, `quick`, `fun`, `again`
- Trophy candidates: `oops`, `nice`, `tiny`, `quick`, `fun`, `again`
- Signature phrasings: `Oops, again.`, `Tiny problem.`, `Nice.`, `Quick question.`
- Example phrases: `Quick question: English?`, `Oops, again please.`
- UI aesthetic: crisp playful accents, quick micro-interactions, compact cards
- Music note: playful electro pop

## Shared Constraints

All vibes must preserve:

- base language: German
- target language: English
- path: English A1 Practical
- A1 practicality
- short repeatable phrases
- respectful beginner-safe tone
- curated trophy words from the vibe palette

The question for every authored line is: would a real A1 learner say this in the situation today?
