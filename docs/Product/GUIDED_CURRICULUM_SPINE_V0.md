# Guided Curriculum Spine V0

Date: 2026-05-12

Guided Today is the practical spine for Resonance language learning. It teaches survival phrases through real moments, not through isolated word lists. The learner is the Traveler: someone walking into rooms, asking for help, paying, choosing, recovering, and leaving with the target language doing useful work.

Free generation remains the playground for learner-chosen topics. Guided Today is what the learner opens when they do not want to decide what comes next. One lesson, one moment, one chosen voice, about ten minutes.

## Product Philosophy

Each lesson is a situation a beginner can meet today: a cafe, a shop, a train, a desk, a reservation, a wrong item, a short wait. Grammar appears only when the moment needs it. Vocabulary appears attached to the place where it is said.

The active voice changes how the Traveler sounds, not what the lesson teaches:

- Bright is warm, open, friendly, and practical.
- Wistful is soft, reflective, careful, and still useful.
- Sharp is direct, crisp, efficient, and polite.

The pedagogical goal, difficulty, session shape, and required production stay stable across vibes.

## Practical Pack Ladder

1. A1 Practical 1 - First Survival Phrases
   First contact, polite follow-up, where questions, simple ordering, price, train, need, like, time, and goodbye.

2. A1 Practical 2 - Small Help and Simple Choices
   Recovery phrases, written help, showing, choices, availability, payment, receipt, reservation, confirmation, and brief waiting.

3. A1 Practical 3 - Moving Around: Places, Time, Transport
   Directions, distance, opening hours, buses, stops, tickets, closing time, landmarks, transport mode, and missed stops.

4. A1 Practical 4 - Food, Cafe, Shop, Small Talk
   Everyday cafe and shop routines, ordering, asking for common items, simple preferences, and small service interactions.

5. A1 Practical 5 - Simple Problems and Plans
   Small mistakes, simple plans, meeting people, names, basic social hooks, and low-pressure coordination.

6. A2 Practical 1 - Two-Turn Interactions
   Short back-and-forth exchanges, follow-ups, simple past reference, and resolving partial answers.

7. A2 Practical 2 - Preferences, Reasons, Constraints
   Likes, reasons, limits, timing, availability, and simple planning tradeoffs.

8. A2 Practical 3 - Polite Disagreement and Alternatives
   Soft disagreement, returns, corrections, substitutions, and practical alternatives.

9. B1 Practical Sample - Scenario Challenge
   A sample extension into sustained scenarios with short opinions, reasons, and connected story fragments.

## Difficulty Ladder

A1 uses short phrases, present tense, concrete nouns, and single practical turns. Production is recognition plus short recall. The learner should be able to say the phrase tomorrow morning.

A2 introduces short sequences, two-turn exchanges, simple past reference, polite pressure, and basic generation. The learner starts following up and resolving partial information.

B1 introduces connected discourse: short stories, brief opinions, reasons, and mixed tense or modal language. The learner produces several connected sentences from a lighter prompt.

The trophy word also grows with level. A1 trophies stay concrete and immediately useful. A2 trophies can become slightly more abstract. B1 trophies can become pragmatic discourse words.

## Session Template Roadmap

All V0 runtime lessons use the current Foundation flow:

Scene -> Match Pairs -> Build Phrase -> Type Recall -> Speak -> Complete

Future templates are content-fit tools, not a rotation:

- Foundation: default for new core phrases in new situations.
- Recall Challenge: removes Match Pairs when the lesson mostly consolidates known material.
- Dialogue Turn: teaches a Traveler line inside a short exchange.
- Choice/Constraint: adds a choice step when the situation is about choosing between valid options.
- Speaking Focus: expands the Speak step for pronunciation-sensitive moments such as names, numbers, or repair phrases.
- Scenario Mission: caps a path with a connected mini-scene using several phrases in sequence.

Recommended rollout: introduce Recall Challenge in a late A1 consolidation lesson, Dialogue Turn in a naturally social or service exchange, Choice/Constraint in a choice lesson, Speaking Focus around numbers/names/repetition, and Scenario Mission as Lesson 10 of a later path after the template has been proven.

## Vibe Content Rules

Bright:

- Warm, friendly, clear, practical.
- Uses generous but adult phrasing.
- Avoid childish excitement and marketing cheer.
- Good trophy direction: lovely, glad, easy, sure, brilliant, splendid, ready.

Wistful:

- Softer, reflective, human, still A1.
- Uses small careful openers and quiet repair.
- Avoid sadness, melodrama, and literary phrasing.
- Good trophy direction: gently, slowly, perhaps, quiet, soft, again, calm, patient.

Sharp:

- Direct, crisp, efficient, polite.
- Uses short intent-first phrasing.
- Avoid rudeness and command-only phrasing.
- Good trophy direction: clear, quick, exactly, ready, certain, straight, focused, direct, settled, done.

For every vibe variant: same situation, same goal, different person speaking.

## A1 Practical 2 Lesson List

1. I don't understand
   Say you do not understand and ask for help.

2. Write it down
   Ask someone to write a name, address, price, or number down.

3. Show me
   Ask someone to show something on a map, phone, or menu.

4. Which one?
   Ask a simple choice question between visible options.

5. Do you have...?
   Ask if something is available.

6. By card
   Ask to pay by card or cash.

7. A receipt, please
   Ask for a receipt or a bag.

8. I have a reservation
   Use a basic booking phrase.

9. Is this right?
   Confirm a bus, train, place, or item.

10. One moment
    Ask someone to wait briefly.
