# Reader directions — 2026-10-05

Owner verdict: **pending**. No direction is approved for integration into the app.

Review all three in the same local reader using the treatment selector. They use
identical real story content and interactions, so the comparison is about reading.

| Option | Composition | What it offers |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas | Dark glass, scene and prose side by side on desktop | Clear relationship between words and explorable details; closest to the existing app |
| Folio | Warm paper within the dark app frame | A quieter book-like reading surface with the same exploration controls |
| Stage | Wide scene over three prose panels | More room for eventual comic art and restrained animation |

Provisional recommendation: **Atlas**. It keeps the text and scene equally visible
and has the smallest gap from the current glassy skin. Mobile stacks the scene
above readable prose. This is an agent recommendation, not the owner's verdict.

All options use a schematic scene placeholder and accessible object chips. The
future illustrations must follow the reviewed art briefs. Stored hotspot
coordinates are not represented as accurate locations on the schematic; the
current chips stay readable independently of those coordinates. No generated art
or animation is claimed. Character voices, image descriptions and narration each
retain separate coordinates in the recording inventory.

The visual study is isolated from the live route and menu. The owner can decide
the final name and placement after trying the reader. Quiz mode, voice recognition
and a new live header item are outside this first reading/listening layer.

Visually verified: yes, in the Codex browser at 1440×1000, 390×844 and 320×800,
including German explanations and long B2 Spanish text. No horizontal overflow
at 320px. Exercised story/level/language selection, all three treatments, object
and line details, vocabulary, page navigation, local resume, completion, and
selection/completion persistence across explanation-language and meaning toggles.
