1. Shift petition. The concession and weekend/overnight burden work. The build allows `주말 교대도 매주` and `매주 주말 교대도`; moving `매주` even to the first clause can describe a coherent repeated burden. `전부는 저도 이해해요. 그래도 ... 반대예요` can concede every reason while opposing the policy, so the fallback should not reject it. Challenge `건 맞아요 / 것은 맞아요`, `그렇다고 해서 / 그렇다고`, `고정하면 / 고정한다면` against what is actually typed.
2. Apartment. The household's childcare objection is fairly answered by three distinct policies. The build offers `낮에만` vs `밤에만`; the working hours support the latter, but the role of `매주`-like adjunct movement matters: `밤에만 공용 세탁실이 비거든요` fits in full. The rules cover use of washers and playing instruments, not literally banning the objects themselves. German and English should express the action. `그래도` and `그렇지만` already both accepted.
3. Café. Specific local prompt, own prices, no national tipping claim. Several normal chip orders, e.g. `화면에서 굳이 팁을 골라야 한다고 생각하지 않아요`, and `팁을 굳이 화면에서 ...`. `굳이 화면으로만 팁을 골라야 한다고 생각하지 않아요` is also compatible with the learner wanting the jar. Regroup chips rather than relying on a verbatim model. German chooses a male barista via `hat er` where Korean does not establish gender. Acknowledge typed `것은 맞아요` and ordinary equivalents, or offer controlled choices.
4. Saturday school. Early introduction of hedged denial in L4 is timely. Avoid asserting academic benefit. Rearrangement `지나치게 늘려서 좋을 거라고 제 생각에는 보기는 어려워요` is marked but possible; `줄여서` is not automatically nonsense, but the preceding proposal and fixed reasons distinguish the direction. Cloze `저도 알아요` permits `저도 이해해요` under concern about falling behind; `줄어들 수도 있어요` allows `줄 수도 있어요`. The exercise should not reject native equivalents.
5. Clinic administrative panel. Strong separation of paperwork/transport from treatment and effectiveness. `질문자분` addresses the person posing questions, yet the administrator asks the respondent on a panel; clarify the role or make the Korean term natural and update caption/English/German. `근거가` is a good trophy here. `점은 인정해요` and `보기는 어려워요` have ordinary contracted/close variants, e.g. `보긴 어려워요`; final `맡자는 거예요` permits `맡자는 거죠` in the same context. Recheck later information.
6. Düsseldorf. The comparison is limited to two distinct cities; no broad car-access policy claim. Both `전면 통제는 현실적으로` and `현실적으로 전면 통제는` can precede `어렵다고 봐요`. Bind adjunct placement. The reply's exceptional permission is a proposal tailored to the fictional council.
7. Singapore. `보고서를 반드시 없애야` and `반드시 보고서를 없애야` work identically. `폐점하면` denotes a shop closing but the situation says a company's office and there is no store/retail operation. Do not teach invariance of produced hours.

Keep the `일지라도` first productive in the second learner reply here.
