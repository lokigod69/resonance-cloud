Start with [P5, making a case](b2/korean/p5.json), follow the [reviewed scene outlines](planning/korean-b2-p5-p10-planning-only.json), or open the [separate practice tasks](assessment/korean-b2). - The [vocabulary allocation](planning/korean-b2-p5-p10-trophies.json) assigns sixty distinct words after checking the previous 340 Korean lessons. P6 has a different conversation partner for the final exchange. The reader identifies both partners and keeps the practice task hidden until all six turns have been revealed.
- French and Brazilian Portuguese each have 100 written B1 lessons. The second half starts at [French P6](b1/french/p6.json) and [Portuguese P6](b1/portuguese/p6.json). P6 covers service updates, P7 explains purpose, and P8 compares earlier habits with life today. P9 works through service problems and P10 brings the year together in personal stories. The offline reader includes German and English meanings for all four turns. Each new path’s index entry links its editorial and independent review records.
- Italian also has 100 written B1 lessons. [P4](b1/italian/p4.json) starts with identifying an object using a relative clause. P5–P9 move through indirect questions, service updates, shared plans, past habits and complaints. In [P10](b1/italian/p10.json) the learner looks back on a year in the neighborhood. The lessons use formal singular *Lei* throughout, with German and English meanings.

The early teaching card models the opening learner turn. This lets a learner try responding to the later challenge before seeing its answer. P7 also names separate participants when a different member of the group actually speaks. A future Today implementation will need to render those roles and support the multi-person data before activating those paths. Each archived lesson keeps the draft schema's pending human review flags. The index links the editorial verdict for the exact text that was checked.

## Continue

Work in ten-lesson paths from the first gap in each language, following that language's actual staged specification. Resolve trophy conflicts before drafting. Write the learner’s first response, the speaker’s new information, the learner’s follow-up and distinct transfer examples; build B2’s six turns around a real objection or constraint. Check whether hidden details become visible in an earlier step. Test all plausible correct answers, including alternatives a learner could assemble by combining distractors. A flat list of target phrases or a path outline does not count as ten lessons.

Korean has full written coverage from A1 through B2. A1 and A2 are available in Today. The offline reader contains all 200 intermediate lessons. Run `npx --no-install tsx content-drafts/text-2026-11/verify-korean-b2.cjs` from the frontend folder to check P5–P10, including word allocation, speaker changes, exercise reconstruction, speaking targets, the P7-to-P9 progression and the sixty practice tasks. Other languages continue from their first missing path in the writing queue. Audio, explanation editions, phrase catalog integration and device grading are separate release steps.
